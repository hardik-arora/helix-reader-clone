The Digital Reading Crisis

THE FRICTION POINTS

  • The Skimming Epidemic: Over 70% of readers scan pages in an F-pattern, missing up to 50% of the core content.
  • The Retention Leak: According to the forgetting curve, 80% of newly read vocabulary is lost within 24 hours without immediate reinforcement.
  • Tool Fragmentation: Readers must jump between files, visual speed apps, and flashcards.

THE HELIX SYSTEM

  • Guided Highlighting: Paced line focus guides align sight paths to double reading speeds.
  • Direct Vocabulary Lock: Double-clicking captures definitions into custom study decks instantly.
  • Narration Engine: Speed-controlled text-to-speech audio outputs reinforce semantic memory.

The Product Pillars

01

Guided Reading Guide

  • Paces the reader through customizable margins and guided guide bars.
  • Reduces eye-regression, helping readers absorb blocks of text systematically.
  • Ideal for long papers and textbooks.
02

Active Vocabulary Deck

  • Replaces standard lookup tabs. Double-click to instantly map definitions.
  • Caches cards automatically into active review collections.
  • Transforms words from passive recognition to active usage.
03

Context Library Hub

  • A customized personal catalog for documents, papers, and EPUBs.
  • Keeps a comprehensive visual log of page completions.
  • Calculates words-per-minute speed in real time.

Target Market & Accessibility

Solving for Diverse Learners

Helix Reader targets critical friction areas in visual learning and active document parsing.

  • Neurodivergent Accessibility: Providing dyslexia-friendly fonts, focus guides, and adjustable speed pacing for readers with ADHD.
  • Life-Long Learners: Helping professionals consume newsletters and learning logs systematically.
