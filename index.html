🎙️ Enable Lifelike Human Voices

Your browser relies on voices downloaded on your Mac. If you only hear robotic synthesizers, follow these quick steps to unlock Apple's premium human voices (Rishi and Isha):

  1. Open your Mac's System Settings.
  2. Go to Accessibility → Spoken Content.
  3. Next to System speech voice, click the voice dropdown and select Manage Voices....
  4. Search for India (for human actors like Rishi/Isha) or Siri.
  5. Click the download icon. Once downloaded, refresh this page and enjoy realistic human narration!
